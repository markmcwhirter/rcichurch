What We Do

Find your place to belong & serve

From worship and prayer to outreach and hands-on service, our ministries are how we live out our mission together — bringing hope, healing, and restoration to our community.

01

Prayer

Experience the transformative power of God's love at Restoration Church's uplifting and inspiring Sunday services. Join us as we come together in worship and fellowship, creating a welcoming and inclusive space for spiritual growth and connection.

02

Community Support

At Restoration Church, we are committed to supporting our community in times of need. Our outreach initiatives aim to provide assistance and resources to those facing challenges, ensuring that everyone feels valued, supported, and embraced by the love of God.

03

Volunteer Opportunities

Explore the various volunteer opportunities at Restoration Church and make a difference in the lives of others. Whether it's serving in the community, assisting with events, or joining our outreach programs, there's a place for you to contribute and serve with love and compassion.

04

Get Involved

Ready to make a lasting impact and spread the message of hope and salvation? Join us in our mission to bring hope, healing, and restoration to individuals and families. Together, we can create a stronger, more connected community.
